# 0.13.6 — Administration permissions and current borrowers

12 November 2026. Apply over the 11 November v0.13.5 reference and run `php artisan optimize:clear`.

- Administrator and Librarian can open Reports and Administration. The latter includes Users, Designations, Units, Borrowing Settings and Member Registration Requests. Audit Logs and Roles & Permissions are Administrator-only.
- Book Activity is available to Administrator and Librarian. Only Administrator sees the link to the full Audit Logs page.
- The staff-facing book details page lists current borrowers and their copy counts below the book information whenever copies are on loan. Returned records are excluded, and older multi-copy loans contribute their recorded quantities. The member portal continues to show a member's own loan status.
